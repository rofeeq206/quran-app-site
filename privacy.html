Privacy

Privacy Policy

The Holy Qur'an app has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your reading stays on your phone.

Last updated: 14 October 2026. Applies to The Holy Qur'an app for Android and iPhone (package com.rofeeq.quranapp) and this website.

In short

  • We do not collect, sell or share personal data, and the app contains no advertising or analytics.
  • Your bookmarks, highlights, reading progress and settings are stored only on your phone.
  • The only information that reaches us is feedback you choose to send.

Stored on your phone only

The app saves your last-read page, bookmarks, verse highlights, display settings (page size and colour), daily goal, reading history and reminder time in the app's private storage on your phone. None of it is sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Daily reminders are scheduled by your phone itself. No server is involved, and you can turn them off at any time on the Progress screen or in your phone's settings.

Internet connections the app makes

The mushaf pages are part of the app and work offline. The app goes online only for:

  • Recitation audio. When you press play, the recitation is streamed from GitHub (github.com). As with any download, GitHub's servers receive your IP address and the file requested; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. The app sends nothing else.
  • Update check (website version only). The Android app downloaded from this website checks this site once when it opens to see whether a newer version exists. No personal information is sent. The Google Play version does not do this; Google Play handles its updates.

Feedback you choose to send

The "Send feedback" link opens our feedback page. If you submit it, we receive your message, the type of feedback, any screenshots you attach, the app version and your phone model and system version, and your name and email address only if you enter them. This is stored with our database provider, Supabase (servers in the European Union), and is used only to fix problems and improve the app. It is never sold or shared. To have your feedback deleted, send a request through the feedback page with the email address you used, or describe the message.

Permissions

  • Notifications: only for the daily reading reminder, and only if you turn it on.
  • Internet: for the recitation audio, as above.
  • Playing in the background: so the recitation continues, with lock-screen controls, when the screen is off.
  • Keep screen awake: so the screen does not turn off while you read.

The app does not use your microphone, camera, contacts, location or files.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, apart from feedback deliberately sent through the feedback page.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions or requests about privacy: use the feedback page and choose "Other".

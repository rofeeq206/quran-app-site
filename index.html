Reading

Page true.
Line for line.

All 601 pages of the Khatt-e-Manzoor, Indo-Pak mushaf. Swipe right for the next page, just like turning a printed copy.

Navigation

Any verse,
in seconds.

Enter chapter and verse, or search a surah by name. The page opens with the verse highlighted.

Highlights

Mark what

Long-press a verse to highlight it in one of four colours.

Every highlight and bookmark waits for you, one tap away.

Daily habit

A little,
every day.

Set a goal of a page or a juz a day. Build a streak and follow your khatm.

A gentle reminder arrives only if you haven’t read yet.

Your last page, bookmarks, highlights and progress are saved only on your device. No accounts.
